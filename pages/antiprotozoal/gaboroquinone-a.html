gaboroquinone A

CHEBI:65940 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An anthraquinone that is anthracene-9,10-dione substituted by a 3-acetyl-4,6-dihydroxy-2-methoxyphenyl moiety at position 1, hydroxy groups at positions 4 and 5 and a hydroxymethyl group at position 2. Isolated from the roots of Bulbine frutescens, it exhibits antiplasmodial and antitrypanosomal activities. — ChEBI
