3-geranyl-2,4,6-trihydroxybenzophenone

CHEBI:65958 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of benzophenones that is benzophenone substituted by a geranyl group at position 3 and hydroxy groups at positions 2, 4 and 6 respectively. Isolated from Leontonyx and Garcinia vieillardii, it exhibits a significant antileishmanial activity. — ChEBI
