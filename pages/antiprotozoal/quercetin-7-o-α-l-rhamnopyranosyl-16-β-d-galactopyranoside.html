quercetin-7-O-[α-L-rhamnopyranosyl(1→6)-β-D-galactopyranoside]

A quercetin O-glycoside that consists of quercetin substituted by a α-L-rhamnopyranosyl(1→6)-β-D-galactopyranosyl residue at position 7 via a glycosidic linkage. Isolated from Grevillea, it exhibits antimalarial activity. — ChEBI
