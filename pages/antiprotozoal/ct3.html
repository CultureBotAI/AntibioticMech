CT3

CHEBI:231816 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of isoindoles that is 2,3-dihydro-1H-isoindole substituted by (3-cyano-1H-1,2,4-triazol-1-yl)acetyl and 5-fluoro-2-(trifluoromethyl)pyridin-3-yl groups at positions 2 and 5, respectively. It inhibits Trypanosoma cruzi topoisomerase II and eradicates Trypanosoma cruzi in a mouse model of Chagas disease. — ChEBI
