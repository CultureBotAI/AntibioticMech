Mechanism summary

Mode of action
FOLATE_PATHWAY_INHIBITION microbial target — CURATOR: Synthetic para-aminobenzenesulfonamide antimicrobial (sulfadiazine) that acts as a competitive structural analog of para-aminobenzoic acid (PABA) at the active site of bacterial dihydropteroate synthase (FolP / DHPS, EC 2.5.1.15), forming dead-end pterin-sulfa adducts and blocking de novo dihydropteroate and tetrahydrofolate biosynthesis (Roland et al. 1979, DOI:10.1128/AAC.15.2.195, PMID:378102; Yun et al. 2012, DOI:10.1126/science.1214641, PMID:22383845).
