dehydrodiconiferyl dibenzoate

CHEBI:65735 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A lignan that is 2,3-dihydro-1-benzofuran substituted by a (benzoyloxy)methyl group at position 3, a 4-hydroxy-3-methoxyphenyl group at position 2, a methoxy group at position 7 and a 3-(benzoyloxy)prop-1-en-1-yl group at position 5. It is isolated as a racemate from Euterpe precatoria and exhibits antiplasmodial activity. — ChEBI
