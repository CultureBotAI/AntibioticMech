amburoside A

CHEBI:65402 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A β-D-glucoside in which a β-D-glucopyranosyl residue is attached at position 4 of 4-hydroxybenzyl 3,4-dihydroxybenzoate via a glycosidic linkage. It is isolated from the trunk barks of Amburana cearensis and exhibits antimalarial, neuroprotective, hepatoprotective and antioxidant activities. — ChEBI
