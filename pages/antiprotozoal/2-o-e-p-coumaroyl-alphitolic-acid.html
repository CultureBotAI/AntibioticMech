2-O-E-p-coumaroyl alphitolic acid

CHEBI:65662 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A pentacyclic triterpenoid that is lup-20(29)-en-28-oic acid substituted by a hydroxy group at position 3 and a [(2E)-3-(4-hydroxyphenyl)prop-2-enoyl]oxy group at position 2 (the 2α,3β-stereoisomer ). It has been isolated from the root bark of Zizyphus jujuba and Ziziphus cambodianus. — ChEBI
