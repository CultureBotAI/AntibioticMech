knipholone

CHEBI:6141 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An anthraquinone that is anthracene-9,10-dione substituted by hydroxy groups at positions 4 and 6, a methyl group at position 2 and a 3-acetyl-2,6-dihydroxy-4-methoxyphenyl group at position 1. It exhibits antioxidant, cytotoxic and antiplasmodial activities. — ChEBI
