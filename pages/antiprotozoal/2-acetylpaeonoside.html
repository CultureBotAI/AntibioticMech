2''-acetylpaeonoside

CHEBI:67929 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A kaempferol O-glucoside that is the 2''-acetyl derivative of kaempferol-3-O-β-D-glucopyranosyl-7-O-β-D-glucopyranoside (paeonoside). Isolated from Delphinium staphisagria, it exhibits trypanocidal activity. — ChEBI
