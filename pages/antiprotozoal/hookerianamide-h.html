hookerianamide H

CHEBI:66020 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A steroid alkaloid that is 5α-pregn-2-ene-4-one substituted by a N-formylamino group at position 3 and a N,N-dimethylamino group at position 20. Isolated from Sarcococca hookeriana, it exhibits antiplasmodial activity and inhibitory activity against cholinesterase. — ChEBI
