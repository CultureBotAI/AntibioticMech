3-(Z)-caffeoyllupeol

CHEBI:65549 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A pentacyclic triterpenoid that is the cinnamate ester obtained by the formal condensation of the carboxylic group of cis-caffeic acid with the hydroxy group of lupeol (the 3β stereoisomer). It is isolated from the fruits of Bruguiera parviflora and exhibits antimalarial activity. — ChEBI
