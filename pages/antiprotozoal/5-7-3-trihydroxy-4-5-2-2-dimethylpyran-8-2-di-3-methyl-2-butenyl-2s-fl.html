5,7,3'-trihydroxy-4',5'-(2'''',2''''-dimethylpyran)-8,2'-di(3-methyl-2-butenyl)-(2S)-flavanone

CHEBI:66267 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An extended flavonoid that consists of (2S)-flavanone substituted by hydroxy groups at positions 5, 7 and 3', prenyl groups at positions 8 and 2' and a gem-dimethyl pyran ring fused across positions 4' and 5'. Isolated from Dendrolobium lanceolatum, it exhibits antimalarial activity. — ChEBI
