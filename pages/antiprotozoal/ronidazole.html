ronidazole

CHEBI:141154 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A carbamate ester that is 5-nitroimidazole in which the hydrogens at positions 1 and 2 are replaced by methyl and (carbamoyloxy)methyl groups, respectively. An antiprotozoal agent, it is used in veterinary medicine for the treatment of histomoniasis and swine dysentery. — ChEBI
