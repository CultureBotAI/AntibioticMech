4'-O-demethylknipholone-4'-O-β-D-glucopyranoside

CHEBI:65741 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An anthraquinone that is knipholone in which the O-methyl group is replaced by a β-D-glucopyranosyl group. It is isolated from the roots of Bulbine frutescens and exhibits trypanocidal and antiplasmodial activities. — ChEBI
