21β-hydroxyolean-12-en-3-one

CHEBI:66049 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A pentacyclic triterpenoid that is olean-12-ene substituted by an oxo substituent at position 3 and a β-hydroxy group at position 21. Isolated from the root barks of Hippocratea excelsa, it exhibits antigiardial activity. — ChEBI
