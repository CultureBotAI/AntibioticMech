1S,4R,6R-1,4-endoperoxy-bisabola-2,10-diene

CHEBI:65844 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A sesquiterpenoid that is bisabola-2,10-diene substituted by a peroxy group between positions 2 and 10 (the 1S,4R,6R stereoisomer). Isolated from Artemisia stolonifera and Eupatorium rufescens, it exhibits antineoplastic and antiplasmodial activity. — ChEBI
