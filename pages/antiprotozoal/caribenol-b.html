caribenol B

CHEBI:65581 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A cyclic terpene ketone that is 2a,3,4,5,5a,6,7,8-octahydroacenaphthylen-1(2H)-one substituted by hydroxy groups at positions 2 and 2a, methyl groups at positions 2, 5 and 8 and a 2-methylprop-1-en-1-yl group at position 3. It is isolated from the the West Indian gorgonian octocoral Pseudopterogorgia elisabethae and exhibits antitubercular and antimalarial activity. — ChEBI
