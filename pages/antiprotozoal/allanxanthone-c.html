allanxanthone C

CHEBI:65387 ·resolve ·ANTIPROTOZOAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of xanthones that is 3,6,8-trihydroxy-1H-xanthene-2,9-dione substituted by geminal prenyl groups at position 1 and a single prenyl group at position 7. It is isolated from the stem barks of Allanblackia monticola and exhibits antiplasmodial activity. It also shows weak cytotoxicity against human melanoma cells. — ChEBI
