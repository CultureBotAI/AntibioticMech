amythiamicin A

antibioticmech:aro-b0e6c47827 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Amythiamicin A is a cyclic thiazolyl peptide antibiotic with a structure similar to GE2270A. Amythiamicin B, C, and D also exist, but are not discussed further here. Amythiamicin A was shown to have the highest activity in cell free protein synthesis assays. — PMID:7706132
