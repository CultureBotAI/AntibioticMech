tuberactinomycin

antibioticmech:aro-4c6e0c1ff0 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Tuberactinomycins are a family of cyclic peptide antibiotics that are important in the treatment of tuberculosis. Tuberactinomycins contain nonproteinogenic amino acids and inhibit group I self-splicing RNA to disrupt prokaryotic protein synthesis. — PMID:7509881
