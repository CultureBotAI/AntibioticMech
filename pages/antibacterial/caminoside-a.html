caminoside A

CHEBI:65570 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A glycolipid with an unusual non-glycerol aglycone, 10-hydroxynonadecan-2-one that is glycosylated at the C10 hydroxy group by a branched tetrasaccharide residue. It is isolated from the marine sponge Caminus sphaeroconia and exhibits potent activity as an inhibitor of a type III bacterial secretory system. — ChEBI
