sulfaphenazole

CHEBI:77780 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A sulfonamide that is sulfanilamide in which the sulfonamide nitrogen is substituted by a 1-phenyl-1H-pyrazol-5-yl group. It is a selective inhibitor of cytochrome P450 (CYP) 2C9 isozyme, and antibacterial agent. — ChEBI
