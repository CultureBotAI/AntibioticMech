UK-69,753

antibioticmech:aro-3e4204a292 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

UK-69,753 is a glycosylated form of factumycin. The disaccharide moiety of UK-69,753 is virtually identical to that of efrotomycin, another member of this class of antibiotics. UK-69,753 has been shown to have strong in vitro and in vivo activity toward a swine pathogen, Treponema hyodysenteriae. — PMID:2584145
