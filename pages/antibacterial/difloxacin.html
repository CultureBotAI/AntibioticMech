difloxacin

CHEBI:4537 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A quinolone that is pefloxacin in which the ethyl group at position 1 of the quinolone has been replaced by a p-fluorophenyl group. A broad-spectrum antibiotic effective against both Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria, it is used (usually as the monohydrochloride salt) for the treatment of bacterial infections in dogs. — ChEBI
