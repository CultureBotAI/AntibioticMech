Molecular targets

The molecular entity or process this compound acts on. Each target carries evidence — this is a mechanistic claim, not a classification.

bacterial energy-coupling factor (ECF) vitamin transporter complex (EcfA1 / EcfA2 / EcfT) PROTEIN_COMPLEX DIRECT_BINDING_TARGET

Inhibition of bacterial energy-coupling factor (ECF) vitamin uptake (IC50 = 4 uM in Lactobacillus casei vitamin transport assays), rapid time-kill bactericidal activity against Enterococcus faecium, whole-cell MICs of 1 to 9 ug/mL against Enterococcus faecalis, Enterococcus faecium, and Streptococcus pneumoniae, and in vivo efficacy in Galleria mellonella and Danio rerio S. pneumoniae infection models (Exapicheidou et al. 2026, DOI:10.1021/acs.jmedchem.5c02721, PMID:42249838). Evidence status: PRIMARY_EVIDENCE. Source: PRIMARY_LITERATURE 2026-01 (retrieved 2026-11-09).

Mechanism summary

Mode of action
