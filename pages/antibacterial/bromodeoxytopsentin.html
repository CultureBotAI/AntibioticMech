bromodeoxytopsentin

CHEBI:144087 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An aromatic ketone that is imidazole which is substituted by a 1H-indole-3-carbonyl group and a 6-bromo-1H-indol-3-yl group at positions 2 and 4, respectively. Isolated from the Mediterranean shallow-water sponge, Topsentia genetrix. It is a potent inhibitor of MRSA pyruvate kinase and exhibits antibacterial properties. — ChEBI
