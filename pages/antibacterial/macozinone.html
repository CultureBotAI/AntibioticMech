macozinone

antibioticmech:curator-607ce000f8 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED PROPOSED

A piperazine-substituted 8-nitro-1,3-benzothiazin-4-one antitubercular agent (PBTZ169) that undergoes nitro-to-nitroso bioreduction and forms a covalent semimercaptal adduct with Cys387 of mycobacterial DprE1. — DOI:10.1002/emmm.201303575
