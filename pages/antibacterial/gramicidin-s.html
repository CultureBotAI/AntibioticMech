gramicidin S

antibioticmech:aro-0734abf918 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Gramicidin S is a cyclical decapeptide with two pentapeptides (Val-Orn-Leu-D-Phe-Pro) joined head to tail. Like other gramicidins, Gramicidin S disrupts membrane permeability of cations while also destabilizing the membrane at higher concentrations. — PMID:10590309
