fluquinometoate

CHEBI:231921 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A quinolone that is quinolin-4(1H)-one bearing cyclopropyl, (2-methoxyethoxy)carbonyl, fluoro and chloro substituents at positions 1, 3, 6 and 7, respectively. It is a bactericide developed by Shandong United Pesticide Industry Co., Ltd that exhibits potent activity against various bacterial pathogens in agriculture. — ChEBI
