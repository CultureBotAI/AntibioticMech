oleandomycin

CHEBI:16869 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

Oleandomycin is a 14-membered macrolide produced by Streptomyces antibioticus. It is ssimilar to erythromycin, and contains a desosamine amino sugar and an oleandrose sugar. It targets the 50S ribosomal subunit to prevent protein synthesis. — PMID:13425352
