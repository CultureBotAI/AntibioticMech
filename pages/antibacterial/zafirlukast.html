zafirlukast

CHEBI:10100 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

An indole-based sulfonylcarbamate (leukotriene receptor antagonist) with direct anti-Gram-positive and antimycobacterial activity mediated by inhibition of staphylococcal lipoteichoic acid synthase (LtaS) and mycobacterial Lsr2. — DOI:10.3390/antibiotics15090914
