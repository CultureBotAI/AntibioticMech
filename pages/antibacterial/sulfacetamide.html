Sulfacetamide competitively inhibits bacterial dihydropteroate synthase (DHPS) to block de novo folate biosynthesis MECHANISTIC

Sulfacetamide (N-[(4-aminophenyl)sulfonyl]acetamide), a synthetic sulfonamide antimicrobial, acts as a para-aminobenzoate (pABA) competitive substrate analog at dihydropteroate synthase (DHPS / FolP), forming a dead-end pterin-sulfa adduct with 6-hydroxymethyl-7,8-dihydropterin pyrophosphate (DHPPP) and blocking de novo folate biosynthesis.
