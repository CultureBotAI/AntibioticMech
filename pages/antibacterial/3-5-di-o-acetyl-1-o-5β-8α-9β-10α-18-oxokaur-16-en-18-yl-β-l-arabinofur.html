An ent-kaurane diterpenoid that is ent-kaur-16-en-18-oic acid substituted by a β-L-3',5'-diacetoxy-arabinofuranosyl moiety at position 18 via a glycosidic linkage. It is isolated from the whole herb of Sagittaria pygmaea and exhibits antibacterial activity against the oral pathogens. — ChEBI
