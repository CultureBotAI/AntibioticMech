cephalexin

CHEBI:3534 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A semisynthetic first-generation cephalosporin antibiotic having methyl and β-(2R)-2-amino-2-phenylacetamido groups at the 3- and 7- of the cephem skeleton, respectively. It is effective against both Gram-negative and Gram-positive organisms, and is used for treatment of infections of the skin, respiratory tract and urinary tract. — ChEBI
