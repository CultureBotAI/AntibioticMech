Molecular targets

The molecular entity or process this compound acts on. Each target carries evidence — this is a mechanistic claim, not a classification.

bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit RIBOSOMAL_SUBUNIT DIRECT_BINDING_TARGET

Binding to 16S rRNA of the bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit by the streptothricin N-glycoside core bearing a tetra-L-beta-lysine chain, inhibiting prokaryotic translation translocation and inducing miscoding (Maruyama et al. 2012, DOI:10.1038/nchembio.1040, PMID:22820420; Inamori et al. 1990, DOI:10.1248/cpb.38.2296, PMID:2177682). Evidence status: PRIMARY_EVIDENCE. Source: PRIMARY_LITERATURE 2012-09 (retrieved 2026-11-06).
