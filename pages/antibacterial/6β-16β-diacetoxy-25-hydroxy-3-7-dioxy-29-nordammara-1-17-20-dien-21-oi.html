6β,16β-diacetoxy-25-hydroxy-3,7-dioxy-29-nordammara-1,17(20)-dien-21-oic acid

CHEBI:65752 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A tetracyclic triterpenoid that is 29-nordammara-1,17(20)-diene substituted by acetoxy groups at positions 6 and 16, a carboxy group at position 21, a hydroxy group at position 25 and oxo groups at positions 3 and 7 respectively. It is isolated from the marine-derived fungal strain Aspergillus sydowii PFW1-13 and exhibits antibacterial activity. — ChEBI
