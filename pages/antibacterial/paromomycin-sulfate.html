paromomycin sulfate

CHEBI:7935 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An aminoglycoside sulfate salt resulting from the treatment of paromomycin with sulfuric acid. A broad-spectrum antibiotic, it is used for the treatment of acute and chronic intestinal protozoal infections, but is not effective for extraintestinal protozoal infections. It is also used as a therapeutic against visceral leishmaniasis. — ChEBI
