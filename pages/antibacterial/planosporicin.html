Molecular targets

The molecular entity or process this compound acts on. Each target carries evidence — this is a mechanistic claim, not a classification.

bacterial cell wall peptidoglycan biosynthesis BIOLOGICAL_PROCESS DOWNSTREAM_AFFECTED_PROCESS

Selective inhibition of bacterial cell-wall peptidoglycan biosynthesis (accumulation of UDP-MurNAc-pentapeptide without immediate membrane depolarization) and whole-cell antibacterial activity against Gram-positive pathogens by the 24-residue pentacyclic type A lantibiotic planosporicin (Castiglione et al. 2007, DOI:10.1021/bi700131x, PMID:17469849; Maffioli et al. 2009, DOI:10.1021/np800794y, PMID:19301921; Sherwood et al. 2013, DOI:10.1128/jb.02291-12, PMID:23475977). Evidence status: PRIMARY_EVIDENCE. Source: PRIMARY_LITERATURE 2013-05 (retrieved 2026-11-09).
