enramycin

antibioticmech:aro-bd55fbe0ca ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Enramycin is a polypeptide antibiotic commonly used as a feed additive for livestock. It is produced by species of the Streptomyces genus and is used to prevent necrotic enteritis in poultry and pigs. Enramycin disrupts cell wall synthesis by inhibiting MurG. — PMID:35841645
