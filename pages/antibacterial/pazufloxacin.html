Pazufloxacin inhibits bacterial DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV to arrest DNA replication MECHANISTIC

Pazufloxacin (T-3761), an aminocyclopropyl fluoroquinolone, inhibits bacterial DNA gyrase (GyrA/GyrB) DNA supercoiling and topoisomerase IV (ParC/ParE) activity, blocking bacterial DNA replication and inducing rapid bactericidal killing.
