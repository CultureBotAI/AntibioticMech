Ozenoxacin dually inhibits bacterial DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV to block bacterial DNA replication MECHANISTIC

Ozenoxacin (T-3912), a nonfluorinated quinolone, dually inhibits bacterial DNA gyrase (GyrA/GyrB) supercoiling and topoisomerase IV (ParC/ParE) decatenation across Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria, retaining potent bactericidal activity against QRDR-mutant strains.
