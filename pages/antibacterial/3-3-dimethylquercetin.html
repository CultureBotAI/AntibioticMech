3,3'-dimethylquercetin

CHEBI:146138 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A dimethoxyflavone that is quercetin in which the hydroxy groups at position 3 and 3' have been replaced by methoxy groups. It has been isolated from several plant species and exhibits anti-bacterial and anti-cancer properties. — ChEBI
