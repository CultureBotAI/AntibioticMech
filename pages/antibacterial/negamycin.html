negamycin

CHEBI:198310 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

Mechanism summary

Mode of action
PROTEIN_SYNTHESIS_INHIBITION microbial target — Pseudodipeptide natural product from Streptomyces purpeofuscus that binds the 16S rRNA helix 34 decoding center of the bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit, stabilizing near-cognate tRNA binding and inducing miscoding (DOI:10.1073/pnas.1414401111; PMID:25368144).

Evidence-backed mechanism graphs: how the compound reaches its target, what the target does, and how the effect propagates to growth inhibition or death.

Negamycin binds 16S rRNA helix 34 and the A-site tRNA anticodon arm to induce miscoding and block translocation MECHANISTIC

Negamycin binds the head domain of the bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit at 16S rRNA helix 34, contacts the sugar-phosphate backbone of the A-site aminoacyl-tRNA anticodon stem-loop, stabilizes near-cognate tRNA occupancy, and impairs mRNA-tRNA translocation and translational fidelity.
