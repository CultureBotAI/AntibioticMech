(−)-marinopyrrole B

CHEBI:66679 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of pyrroles that is 1'H-1,3'-bipyrrole substituted by a bromo group at position 3, four chloro groups at positions 4, 4', 5 and 5' and two 2-hydroxybenzoyl moieties at positions 2 and 2'. It is isolated from Streptomyces sp.CNQ-418 and exhibits cytotoxic and antibacterial activities. — ChEBI
