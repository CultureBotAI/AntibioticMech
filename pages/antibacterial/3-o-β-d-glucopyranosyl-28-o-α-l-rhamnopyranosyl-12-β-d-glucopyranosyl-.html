3-O-[β-D-glucopyranosyl]-28-O-[α-L-rhamnopyranosyl-(1→2)-β-D-glucopyranosyl]maslinic acid

CHEBI:68377 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A triterpenoid saponin that is maslinic acid attached to a β-D-glucopyranosyl residue at position 3 and a α-L-rhamnopyranosyl-(1→2)-β-D-glucopyranosyl residue at position 28 via a glycosidic linkage. Isolated from the methanolic extract of the leaves of Symplocos lancifolia, it exhibits antibacterial activity. — ChEBI
