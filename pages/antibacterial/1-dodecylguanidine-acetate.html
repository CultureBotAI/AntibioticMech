1-dodecylguanidine acetate

CHEBI:74889 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An acetate salt resulting from the reaction of equimolar amounts of 1-dodecylguanidine and acetic acid. It is used as a fungicide to control black spot and foliar diseases on apples, pears, peaches, nectarines and strawberries. — ChEBI
