teicoplanin

antibioticmech:aro-a71ef91d49 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Teicoplanin is a glycopeptide antibiotic used in the prophylaxis and treatment of serious infections caused by Gram-positive bacteria. Teicoplanin has a unique acyl-aliphatic chain, and binds to cell wall precursors to inhibit transglycosylation and transpeptidation. — PMID:16323116
