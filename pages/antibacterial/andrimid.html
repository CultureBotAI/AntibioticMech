bacterial acetyl-CoA carboxylase carboxyltransferase complex (AccA / AccD) PROTEIN_COMPLEX DIRECT_BINDING_TARGET

GO:0009317

Competitive and sub-nanomolar/nanomolar inhibition of the purified Escherichia coli and Staphylococcus aureus acetyl-CoA carboxylase (ACC) carboxyltransferase reaction (AccA/AccD; Ki = 5 nM against E. coli ACC), selective arrest of whole-cell bacterial fatty acid biosynthesis ([14C]acetate incorporation), and target validation via spontaneous resistance mutations in accA and accD and AccA/AccD overexpression (Freiberg et al. 2004, DOI:10.1074/jbc.m402989200, PMID:15066985; Jin et al. 2006, DOI:10.1021/ja063194c, PMID:16866529).
