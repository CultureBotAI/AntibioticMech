phenelfamycin E

antibioticmech:aro-0ae735fe08 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Phenelfamycin E was isolated from two environmental isolates called AB999F-80 and AB1047T-33. They were both identified as Streptomyces violaceoniger strains. Both these strains were found to produce phenelfamycins A-F and unphenelfamycin. Phenelfamycin E has a trisaccaride moeity and is isomeric with phenelfamycin F. All phenelfamycins were selected due to their activity against anaerobic bacteria, especially Clostridioides difficile. Phenelfamycin E and F are the most potent of the phenelfamycins. — PMID:3192490
