Molecular targets

bacterial duplex DNA NUCLEIC_ACID DIRECT_BINDING_TARGET

CHEBI:16991

Thiol-triggered Bergman cycloaromatization of the nine-membered enediyne chromophore to a 2,6-dehydro-p-indacene diradical that abstracts hydrogen atoms from deoxyribose in the minor groove of bacterial duplex DNA, causing single- and double-strand DNA cleavage and inhibiting DNA synthesis in Sarcina lutea (Micrococcus luteus) and Staphylococcus aureus (Liu et al. 2005, DOI:10.1016/j.chembiol.2004.12.013, PMID:15797213; Ohtsuki & Ishida 1975, DOI:10.7164/antibiotics.28.229, PMID:123897).
