TNP-2198 is an experimental rifamycin-nitroimidazole conjugate currently in development for treatment of several bacterial infections. It exhibits greater activity than rifamycin and nitroimidazole antibiotics administered together and is effective against bacteria resistant to both drug classes. — PMID:35175750
