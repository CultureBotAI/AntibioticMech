Mechanism summary

Mode of action
PROTEIN_SYNTHESIS_INHIBITION microbial target — CURATOR: Thiazoline-containing siderophore-like polyketide-nonribosomal peptide antibiotic from Ralstonia/Pseudomonas sp. that chelates divalent transition-metal cations (Zn2+, Cu2+, Fe3+) and inhibits bacterial protein biosynthesis and transition-metal homeostasis, with potent antimycoplasmal activity (Kobayashi et al. 1998, DOI:10.7164/antibiotics.51.323, PMID:9589068; Ino et al. 1998, DOI:10.7164/antibiotics.51.328).
