Sitafloxacin

CHEBI:4304 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

Sitafloxacin is a fluoroquinolone active against multi-resistant Gram-positive and negative pathogens. Sitafloxacin shows inhibitory activity against DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV, which blocks bacterial DNA replication, thereby causing double-stranded breaks in the bacterial chromosome. — PMID:21504249
