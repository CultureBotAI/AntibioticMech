cephapirin

CHEBI:554446 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A cephalosporin with acetoxymethyl and 2(pyridin-4-ylsulfanyl)acetamido substituents at positions 3 and 7, respectively, of the cephem skeleton. It is used (as its sodium salt) as an antibiotic, being effective against gram-negative and gram-positive organisms. — ChEBI
