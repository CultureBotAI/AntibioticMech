Molecular targets

bacterial peptidoglycan precursor lipid II CELL_WALL_COMPONENT DIRECT_BINDING_TARGET

Complexation with the membrane-bound peptidoglycan precursor lipid II by the chlorinated/hydroxylated lantibiotic microbisporicin A2 (NAI-107 component), blocking nascent cell-wall peptidoglycan biosynthesis with potent Gram-positive antibacterial activity (Castiglione et al. 2008, DOI:10.1128/aac.01716-07, PMID:18490500; Foulston and Bibb 2010, DOI:10.1073/pnas.1008285107, PMID:20628010).
