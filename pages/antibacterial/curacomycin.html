curacomycin

CHEBI:156426 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A homodetic cyclic peptide found in several Streptomyces species, consisting of 3-hydroxyasparagine, L-ornithine, L-isoleucine, D-leucine, D-valine and 5-chloro-L-tryptophan joined in sequence in a cyclic arrangement by peptide linkages. It exhibits antibacterial properties. — ChEBI
