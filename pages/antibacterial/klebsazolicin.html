klebsazolicin

antibioticmech:aro-2b2f71621c ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Klebsazolicin is a peptide antibiotic encoded by and identified from the genome of Klebsiella pneumoniae. Klebsazolicin is a ribosomally synthesized and post-translationally modified peptide with antibiotic activity against gram-negative bacteria. Klebsazolicin inhibits ribosomal protein synthesis through binding and thus blocking peptide access. — PMID:28846667
