Across all 304 baseline C. difficile isolates collected in the Phase 3 Ri-CoDIFy trials, ridinilazole demonstrated potent in vitro activity with an MIC50 of 0.125 mg/L, an MIC90 of 0.25 mg/L, and an MIC range of 0.03 to 0.5 mg/L."
