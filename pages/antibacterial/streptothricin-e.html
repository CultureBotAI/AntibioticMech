bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit RIBOSOMAL_SUBUNIT DIRECT_BINDING_TARGET

Binding to 16S rRNA of the bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit by the streptothricin N-glycoside core bearing a di-L-beta-lysine chain, inhibiting prokaryotic translation translocation and inducing miscoding (Inamori et al. 1990, DOI:10.1248/cpb.38.2296, PMID:2177682; Maruyama et al. 2012, DOI:10.1038/nchembio.1040, PMID:22820420).
