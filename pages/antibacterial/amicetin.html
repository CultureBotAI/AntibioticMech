Binding of the disaccharide cytosine nucleoside antibiotic amicetin to the 50S ribosomal subunit peptidyl-transferase center to inhibit peptide-bond formation and bacterial protein synthesis in mycobacteria and Gram-positive/Gram-negative bacteria (DeBoer et al. 1953, DOI:10.1021/ja01098a518; Zhang et al. 2012, DOI:10.1128/aem.07185-11, PMID:22267658).
