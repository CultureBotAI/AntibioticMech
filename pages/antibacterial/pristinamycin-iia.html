pristinamycin IIA

CHEBI:9997 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A macrolide that is (together with pristinamycin IA) a component of pristinamycin, an oral streptogramin antibiotic produced by Streptomyces pristinaespiralis. Pristinamycin exhibits bactericidal activity against Gram positive organisms including methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus. — ChEBI
