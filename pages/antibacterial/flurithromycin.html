flurithromycin

CHEBI:131719 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An erythromycin derivative that is erythromycin A in which the hydrogen attached to the carbon at position 8 (α to the ketone carbonyl group) has been replaced by a fluorine. It has been used (generally as the corresponding monoethyl succinate ester) as an antibacterial drug. — ChEBI
