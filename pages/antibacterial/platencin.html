platencin

CHEBI:68241 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A monocarboxylic acid amide obtained by the formal condensation of the amino group of 3-amino-2,4-dihydroxybenzoic acid with the carboxy group of the polycyclic cage component. It is an antibiotic isolated from Streptomyces platensis and exhibits inhibitory activity against fatty acid synthase. — ChEBI
