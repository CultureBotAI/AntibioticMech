gramicidin D

antibioticmech:aro-edfe06b075 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Gramicidin D is a mixture of the linear peptides gramicidin A, B, and C, each with 15 alternating L- and D-amino acids. They are active against most gram-positive bacteria and select gram-negative bacteria. These compounds create channels in the bacterial membrane and increase the permeability to cations. — PMID:9672588
