viridicatin

CHEBI:140443 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A hydroxyquinolone that is 3-hydroxyuinolin-2(1H)-one which is substituted at position 4 by a phenyl groups. Isolated from the mycelium of several Penicillium species, it exhibits strong antibiotic activity against M. tuberculosis and also against B. subtilis, S. aureus and S. cerevisiae. — ChEBI
