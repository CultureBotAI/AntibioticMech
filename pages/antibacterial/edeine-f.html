edeine F

antibioticmech:aro-43392693fd ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Edeine F is a subtype of edeine similar to edeine B with beta-tyr replaced by beta-phe-beta-ala. Edeines bind to the 30S subunit to block fMet-tRNA interaction at the P site, inhibiting protein synthesis and subsequent structure/function processes critical for life or replication. — PMID:6615592
