fumagillin

CHEBI:48635 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A meroterpenoid resulting from the formal condensation of the hydroxy group of fumagillol with the carboxylic acid group of (all-E)-deca-2,4,6,8-tetraenedioic acid. Originally isolated from the fungus Aspergillus fumigatus, it is used for the control of Nosema infection in honey bees. — ChEBI
