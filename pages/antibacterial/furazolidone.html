furazolidone

CHEBI:5195 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of oxazolidines that is 1,3-oxazolidin-2-one in which the hydrogen attached to the nitrogen is replaced by an N-{[(5-nitro-2-furyl)methylene]amino} group. It has antibacterial and antiprotozoal properties, and is used in the treatment of giardiasis and cholera. — ChEBI
