cinoxacin

CHEBI:3716 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of cinnolines that is 6,7-methylenedioxycinnolin-4(1H)-one bearing an ethyl group at position 1 and a carboxylic acid group at position 3. An analogue of oxolinic acid, it has similar antibacterial actions. It was formerly used for the treatment of urinary tract infections. — ChEBI
