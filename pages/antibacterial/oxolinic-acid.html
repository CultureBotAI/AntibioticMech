oxolinic acid

CHEBI:138856 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A quinolinemonocarboxylic acid having the carboxy group at position 7 as well as oxo and ethyl groups at positions 4 and 1 respectively and a dioxolo ring fused at the 5- and 6-positions. A synthetic antibiotic, it is used in veterinary medicine for the treatment of bacterial infections in cattle, pigs and poultry. — ChEBI
