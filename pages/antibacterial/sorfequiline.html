TBAJ-876 displayed submicromolar in vitro activity against reference strains representing the three subspecies of M. abscessus and against a collection of clinical isolates." (Reported by Sarathy et al. 2020 (Antimicrob Agents Chemother 64:e02404-19; PMID:31964791) and Sarathy et al. 2019 (Antimicrob Agents Chemother 63:e01191-19; PMID:31358589; DOI:10.1128/aac.01191-19) as a next-generation 3,5-dialkoxypyridine diarylquinoline (TBAJ-876, INN sorfequiline) targeting the c and epsilon subunits of mycobacterial F1FO-ATP synthase, with MIC50 = 0.004 uM against Mycobacterium tuberculosis H37Rv and submicromolar MICs (0.15-0.6 uM) across Mycobacterium abscessus isolates.)
