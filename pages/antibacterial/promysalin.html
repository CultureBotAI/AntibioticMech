promysalin

CHEBI:171658 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A carboxylic ester resulting from the formal condensation of the carboxy group of (2S)-1-(2-hydroxybenzoyl)-2,3-dihydro-1H-pyrrole-2-carboxylic acid with the hydroxy group of (2R,8R)-2,8-dihydroxytetradecanamide. It is a metabolite isolated from Pseudomonas putida, , which resides in the rhizosphere of rice plants. The compound selectively inhibits the growth of Pseudomonas aeruginosa at low-micromolar concentrations. — ChEBI
