formic acid

CHEBI:30751 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

The simplest carboxylic acid, containing a single carbon. Occurs naturally in various sources including the venom of bee and ant stings, and is a useful organic synthetic reagent. Principally used as a preservative and antibacterial agent in livestock feed. Induces severe metabolic acidosis and ocular injury in human subjects. — ChEBI
