Bacteria NCBITaxon:2

  • DOI:10.1128/AAC.40.9.2142 — "Singh et al. reported that the tricyclic beta-lactam (trinem) sanfetrinem (GV104326) exhibited potent broad-spectrum bactericidal activity against Gram-positive, Gram-negative, and anaerobic bacteria by inhibiting bacterial penicillin-binding proteins and cell wall synthesis." (Singh et al. 1996 (PMID:8878596) and Di Modugno et al. 1994 (DOI:10.1128/AAC.38.10.2362, PMID:7840571) established that GV104326 (sanfetrinem) binds essential bacterial PBPs across Gram-positive and Gram-negative pathogens and resists hydrolysis by common beta-lactamases.)
