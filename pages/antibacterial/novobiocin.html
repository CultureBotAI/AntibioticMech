Novobiocin inhibits GyrB ATPase-dependent DNA supercoiling MECHANISTIC

Novobiocin binds the amino-terminal coumarin-binding region of bacterial DNA gyrase subunit GyrB, competitively inhibits its ATPase activity, and blocks ATP-dependent DNA supercoiling by gyrase.
