Salinamide B, a chlorinated bicyclic depsipeptide antibiotic from marine Streptomyces sp. CNB-091, binds the bacterial DNA-directed RNA polymerase (RpoB/RpoC) bridge-helix cap/hinge pocket (PDB:4MEY), allosterically blocking bridge-helix N-terminal hinge unbending required for nucleotide addition and arresting bacterial transcription.
