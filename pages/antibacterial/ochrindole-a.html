ochrindole A

CHEBI:167043 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A bisindole alkaloid that is phenol bearing a 3,3-dimethylallyl group at position 4, two methoxy groups at positions 3 and 6, and two indol-3-yl groups at positions 2 and 5. It is a natural product isolated from the sclerotia of Aspergillus orbraceus, with moderate activity against some insects, as well as Gram-positive bacteria. — ChEBI
