Prulifloxacin

CHEBI:32071 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

Prulifloxacin is a 6-fluoroquinolone antibiotic with a C-2 sulfur atom. It is hydrolyzed by esterases to produce the active compound Ulifloxacin. The active compound has broad spectrum activity against most Gram-negative bacteria. Prulifloxacin displays strong bactericidal activity against Pseudomonas aeruginosa biofilms and persister cells. — PMID:1667252
