Besifloxacin inhibits bacterial DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV with balanced dual-target potency to block DNA replication MECHANISTIC

Besifloxacin (BOL-303224-A), an 8-chloro-fluoroquinolone, potently and equipotently inhibits both bacterial DNA gyrase (GyrA/GyrB) DNA supercoiling and topoisomerase IV (ParC/ParE) decatenation in Gram-positive and Gram-negative pathogens, blocking bacterial DNA replication.
