methylene bis(thiocyanate)

CHEBI:134614 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of thiocyanates that is methane in which two of the hycrogens have been replaced by thiocyanato groups. Used as a biocide for the control of various pathogens and algae in industrial water systems and as a fungicide for the control of surface moulds and sapstain fungi on wood. — ChEBI
