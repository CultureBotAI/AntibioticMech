klebsidin

antibioticmech:aro-1d2367825f ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Klebsidin is a peptide antibiotic derived from Klebsiella pneumoniae that exhibits activity against members of the Enterobacteriaceae family of Gram-negative bacteria, including Escherichia and Salmonella species. Klebsidin acts by accumulating in the target cell and inhibiting RNA polymerase. — PMID:35315272
