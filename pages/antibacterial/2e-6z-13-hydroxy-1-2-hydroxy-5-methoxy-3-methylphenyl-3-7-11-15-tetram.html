A meroterpenoid that is hexadeca-2,6,14-triene-5,12-dione substituted by a hydroxy group at position 13, a 2-hydroxy-5-methoxy-3-methylphenyl group at position 1 and methyl groups at positions 3, 7, 11 and 15.
