iodoquinol

CHEBI:5950 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A monohydroxyquinoline that is quinolin-8-ol in which the hydrogens at positions 5 and 7 are replaced by iodine. It is considered the drug of choice for treating asymptomatic or moderate forms of amoebiasis. — ChEBI
