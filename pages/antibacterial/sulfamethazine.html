Sulfamethazine competitively inhibits bacterial dihydropteroate synthase (DHPS) to block de novo folate biosynthesis MECHANISTIC

Sulfamethazine (4-amino-N-(4,6-dimethylpyrimidin-2-yl)benzenesulfonamide), a synthetic sulfonamide antimicrobial, acts as a para-aminobenzoate (pABA) competitive substrate analog at dihydropteroate synthase (DHPS / FolP), forming a dead-end pterin-sulfa adduct with 6-hydroxymethyl-7,8-dihydropterin pyrophosphate (DHPPP) and blocking de novo folate biosynthesis.
