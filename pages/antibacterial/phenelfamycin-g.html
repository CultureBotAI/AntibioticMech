phenelfamycin G

antibioticmech:aro-7086196093 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Phenelfamycin G is produced by Streptomyces albospinus strain Acta 3619. Phenelfamycin G and H are isomeric and similar to phenelfamycin E and F only with the addition of a hydroxyl group at position C-30. Phenelfamycin G and H have a narrow antibacterial spectrum with potent activity against Cutibacterium acnes. — PMID:21285962
