fumimycin

CHEBI:65931 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of 1-benzofurans that is 1-benzofuran-2(3H)-one substituted by a propenyl group at position 4, hydroxy groups at positions 5 and 6, methyl group at position 3 and a [(2E)-3-carboxyprop-2-enoyl]nitrilo group at position 3.
