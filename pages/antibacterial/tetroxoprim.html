Tetroxoprim selectively inhibits bacterial dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR) to block tetrahydrofolate and nucleotide biosynthesis MECHANISTIC

Tetroxoprim (5-[3,5-dimethoxy-4-(2-methoxyethoxy)benzyl]pyrimidine-2,4-diamine), a synthetic 2,4-diaminopyrimidine antibacterial, selectively binds and inhibits bacterial dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR / FolA), blocking NADPH-dependent reduction of 7,8-dihydrofolate to 5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate and arresting folate-dependent thymidylate and purine biosynthesis.
