butirosin

antibioticmech:aro-63094b52cf ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Butirosin is an aminoglycoside antibiotic used to treat different types of bacterial infections. Butirosin works by binding to the bacterial 30S ribosomal subunit, causing misreading of mRNA and leaving the bacterium unable to synthesize proteins vital to its growth. — PMID:10103173
