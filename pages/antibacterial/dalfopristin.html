dalfopristin

CHEBI:4309 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

A macrolide that is pristinamycin IIA in which the double bond between positions 26 and 26a of the pyrroline ring has been reduced and position 26R carries a [2-(diethylamino)ethyl]sulfonyl group. It is a semi-synthetic streptogramin antibiotic and often used as a mixture with quinupristin for the treatment of vancomycin-resistant Enterococcus faecium. — ChEBI

Mechanism summary

Mode of action
PROTEIN_SYNTHESIS_INHIBITION host shared target — CURATOR: PMID:15059283 resolved dalfopristin with quinupristin on the Deinococcus radiodurans 50S subunit. Dalfopristin binds in the peptidyl-transferase center, perturbs A- and P-site tRNA occupation, and cooperates with quinupristin to stabilize a nonproductive rRNA conformation. PMID:12771141 also resolved dalfopristin in the resistance acetyltransferase VatD.
