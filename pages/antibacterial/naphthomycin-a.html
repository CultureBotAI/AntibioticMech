Mechanism summary

Mode of action
NUCLEIC_ACID_SYNTHESIS_INHIBITION microbial target — CURATOR: Ansamycin antibiotic from Streptomyces collinus that inhibits bacterial nucleic acid synthesis and acts as a sulfhydryl-reactive vitamin K antagonist in Gram-positive bacteria; cysteine and other sulfhydryl compounds reverse growth inhibition by nucleophilic Michael addition to the naphthoquinone chromophore (Balerna et al. 1969, DOI:10.1007/BF00409749, PMID:4978971; Okabe et al. 1985, DOI:10.7164/antibiotics.38.230, PMID:3997669).
