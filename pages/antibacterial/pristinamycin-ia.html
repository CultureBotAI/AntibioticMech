pristinamycin IA

CHEBI:8417 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A cyclodepsipeptide that is (together with pristinamycin IIA) a component of pristinamycin, an oral streptogramin antibiotic produced by Streptomyces pristinaespiralis. Pristinamycin exhibits bactericidal activity against Gram positive organisms including methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus. — ChEBI
