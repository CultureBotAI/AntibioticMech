bleomycin

antibioticmech:aro-1504589de3 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Bleomycin is a family of glycopeptide antibiotics produced by the bacterium Streptomyces verticillus. Bleomycins, taken as a mixture, act by the induction of DNA and RNA strand breaks. In addition to its antibacterial activity, bleomycin is also used as an anticancer agent. — PMID:15700963
