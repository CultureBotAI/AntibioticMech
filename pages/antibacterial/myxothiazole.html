myxothiazole

CHEBI:25461 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

This chemical compound is produced by the myxobacterium Myxococcus fulvus. It is an inhibitor of the mitochondrial cytochrome bc1 complex (coenzyme Q - cytochrome c reductase). It is a competitive inhibitor of ubiquinol, and binds at the quinol oxidation (Qo) site of the bc1 complex, blocking electron transfer to the Rieske iron-sulfur protein. — PMID:2547800
