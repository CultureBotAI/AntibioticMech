epidermin

CHEBI:71659 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A type A lantibiotic that is a tetracyclic 21-amino-acid peptide which contains meso-lanthionine, 3-methyllanthionine, and S-(2-aminovinyl)-D-cysteine. It is obtained from Staphylococcus epidermidis Tü 3298 and exhibits antibiotic activity against many Gram-positive bacteria. — ChEBI
