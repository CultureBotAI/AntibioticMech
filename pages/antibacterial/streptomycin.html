Mechanism summary

Mode of action
PROTEIN_SYNTHESIS_INHIBITION microbial target — CURATOR: PMID:23322043 resolved streptomycin at the Thermus thermophilus 30S decoding center with cognate or near-cognate anticodon-stem-loop and mRNA substrates. Streptomycin perturbs the bacterial 30S decoding site MECHANISTIC

Streptomycin binds the bacterial 30S ribosomal decoding site formed by 16S rRNA and ribosomal protein S12, distorts 16S rRNA helix 44, and lowers translational decoding accuracy.
