chaetoglocin B

CHEBI:68767 ·resolve ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of 2-pyranones that is 2H-pyran-2-one substituted by a (2Z)-1-hydroxybut-2-en-2-yl group at position 6, a hydroxymethyl group at position 5 and a methoxy group at position 4.
