edeine D

antibioticmech:aro-1cc6dbd596 ·ANTIBACTERIAL ·MINTED SEEDED

Edeine D is a subtype of edeine similar to edeine A with the beta-tyr replaced by beta-phe-beta-ala. Edeines bind to the 30S subunit to block fMet-tRNA interaction at the P site, inhibiting protein synthesis and subsequent structure/function processes critical for life or replication. — PMID:70202
