Antimicrobial chemical structures, harmonized

One AntibioticRecord is one chemical structure — a compound with a defined connectivity (an InChIKey), not a drug class. "Macrolide antibiotic" is a structural class and appears as structural_class on the records it covers; erythromycin A is a record. AntibioticMech merges ChEBI's antimicrobial role hierarchy with CARD's Antibiotic Resistance Ontology (ARO) molecule list onto one identity per structure, and layers on the mechanism a compound list does not carry: molecular target, mode of action, and resistance route.

Identity grounding

Every record's identity is either an ontology term asserted by ChEBI or ARO themselves, or — when no defensible term exists — a content-hashed antibioticmech: CURIE minted at seed time. Grounding is a fact about the identifier, kept separately from curator review.
