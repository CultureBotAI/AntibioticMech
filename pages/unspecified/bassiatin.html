bassiatin

CHEBI:65465 ·resolve ·ANTIMICROBIAL_UNSPECIFIED ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of morpholines that is morpholine-2,5-dione substituted by a benzyl, isopropyl and a methyl group at positions 3, 6 and 4 respectively. It is isolated from the culture broth of the fungus Beauveria bassiana and acts as a platelet aggregation inhibitor. — ChEBI
