cathestatin B

CHEBI:65600 ·resolve ·ANTIMICROBIAL_UNSPECIFIED ·EXACT SEEDED

A carboxamide obtained by the formal condensation of the carboxylic group of oxirane-2,3-dicarboxylic acid with the amino group of N-(4-aminobutyl)-L-tyrosinamide (the 2S,3S stereoisomer). An antibiotic isolated from the fermentation broth of Penicillium citrinum, it acts as a potent inhibitor of cysteine protease. — ChEBI
