clopidogrel

CHEBI:37941 ·resolve ·ANTIMICROBIAL_UNSPECIFIED ·EXACT SEEDED

A thienopyridine that is 4,5,6,7-tetrahydrothieno[3,2-c]pyridine in which the hydrogen attached to the nitrogen is replaced by an o-chlorobenzyl group, the methylene hydrogen of which is replaced by a methoxycarbonyl group (the S enantiomer). A P2Y12 receptor antagonist, it is used to inhibit blood clots and prevent heart attacks. — ChEBI
