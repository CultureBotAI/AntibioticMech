amrubicin

CHEBI:135779 ·resolve ·ANTIMICROBIAL_UNSPECIFIED ·EXACT SEEDED

A synthetic anthracycline antibiotic with molecular formula C25H25NO9. A specific inhibitor of topoisomerase II, it is used (particularly as the hydrochloride salt) in the treatment of cancer, especially lung cancer, where it is a prodrug for the active metabolite, ambrucinol. — ChEBI
