6-aminopenicillanic acid

CHEBI:16705 ·resolve ·ANTIMICROBIAL_UNSPECIFIED ·EXACT SEEDED

A penicillanic acid compound having a (6R)-amino substituent. The active nucleus common to all penicillins, it may be substituted at the 6-amino position to form the semisynthetic penicillins, resulting in a variety of antibacterial and pharmacologic characteristics. — ChEBI
