5-[[3-(1-phenylethoxy)-4-(2-phenylethoxy)phenyl]methylene]-4-oxo-2-thioxo-3-thiazolidineacetic acid

CHEBI:156434 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of thiazolidinones that is 2-sulfanylidene-1,3-thiazolidin-4-one in which the hydrogens at positions 3 and 5 are replaced by carboxymethyl and 3-(1-phenylethoxy)-4-(2-phenylethoxy)benzylidene groups, respectively. It is an inhibitor of protein mannosyl transferase 1 (PMT1) and blocks protein O-mannosylation. — ChEBI
