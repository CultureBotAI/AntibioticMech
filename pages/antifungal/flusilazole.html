flusilazole

CHEBI:81922 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An organosilicon compound that is dimethylsilane in which the hydrogens attached to the silicon are replaced by p-fluorophenyl groups and a hydrogen attached to one of the methyl groups is replaced by a 1H-1,2,4-triazol-1-yl group. It is a broad-sepctrum fungicide used to protect a variety of crops. — ChEBI
