carvacrol

CHEBI:3440 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A phenol that is a natural monoterpene derivative of cymene. An inhibitor of bacterial growth, it is used as a food additive. Potent activator of the human ion channels transient receptor potential V3 (TRPV3) and A1 (TRPA1). — ChEBI
