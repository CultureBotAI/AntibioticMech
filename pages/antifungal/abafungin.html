abafungin

CHEBI:76005 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of guanidines that is tetrahydropyrimidin-2(1H)-imine in which the hydrogen of the imino group is replaced by a thiazol-2-yl group which in turn is substituted by a 2-(2,4-dimethylphenoxy)phenyl group at position 4. It has been used for the topical treatment of fungal nail infections. — ChEBI
