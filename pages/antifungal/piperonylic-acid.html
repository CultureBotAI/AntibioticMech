piperonylic acid

CHEBI:107644 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of benzodioxoles that is 1,3-benzodioxole substituted by a carboxy group at position 5. It is a natural product isolated from several plant species. It is a selective mechanism-based inactivator of the trans-cinnamate 4-hydroxylase enzyme and exhibits antifungal and skin wound healing properties. — ChEBI
