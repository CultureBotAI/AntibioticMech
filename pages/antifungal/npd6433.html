An indolyl carboxylate ester that is 1H-indole substituted by methoxycarbonyl and (E)-(8-oxo-1,5,6,8-tetrahydro-2H-1,5-methanopyrido[1,2-a][1,5]diazocin-3(4H)-yl)diazenyl groups at positions 2 and 3, respectively.
