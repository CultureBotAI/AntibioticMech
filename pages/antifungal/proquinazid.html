proquinazid

CHEBI:83555 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of quinazolines that is quinazolin-4-one substituted at positions 2, 3 and 6 by propoxy, propyl and iodo groups respectively. A fungicide with the potential to control powdery mildew in a range of crops including cereals and grapes. — ChEBI
