oligomycin A

CHEBI:28285 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

An oligomycin with formula C45H74011. An inhibitor of mitochondrial F1FO ATP synthase that induces apoptosis in a variety of cell types and exhibits antifungal, antitumour, and nematicidal activities, but its clinical application has been limited by poor solubility in water and other biocompatible solvents. — ChEBI
