30S ribosomal subunit mRNA channel RIBOSOMAL_SUBUNIT DIRECT_BINDING_TARGET

X-ray structures of kasugamycin with the E. coli 70S ribosome and with the 30S subunit directly located two drug-binding sites; paired biochemical experiments tested effects on initiation. Evidence status: PRIMARY_EVIDENCE. Source: PRIMARY_LITERATURE 2006-09 (retrieved 2026-09-02).

Mode of action

Kasugamycin binds the 30S ribosomal mRNA channel to block canonical translation initiation MECHANISTIC

Kasugamycin binds within the mRNA path of the Escherichia coli 30S ribosomal subunit near the P/E-site junction, perturbs the mRNA-initiator-tRNA codon-anticodon interaction, and inhibits canonical bacterial translation initiation.
