carboxin

CHEBI:3405 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An anilide obtained by formal condensation of the amino group of aniline with the carboxy group of 2-methyl-5,6-dihydro-1,4-oxathiine-3-carboxylic acid. A fungicide for control of bunts and smuts that is normally used as a seed treatment. — ChEBI
