cycloheximide

CHEBI:27641 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A dicarboximide that is 4-(2-hydroxyethyl)piperidine-2,6-dione in which one of the hydrogens attached to the carbon bearing the hydroxy group is replaced by a 3,5-dimethyl-2-oxocyclohexyl group. It is an antibiotic produced by the bacterium Streptomyces griseus. — ChEBI
