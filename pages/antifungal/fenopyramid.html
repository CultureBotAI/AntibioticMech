fenopyramid

CHEBI:229753 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A secondary carboxamide resulting from the formal condensation of the carboxy group of 3-(difluoromethyl)-1-methyl-1H-pyrazole-4-carboxylic acid with the amino group of 2-[2,4-bis(trifluoromethyl)phenoxy]aniline. It is a fungicide developed by Shandong United Pesticide Industry Co. that demonstrates effective control against soybean rust, corn rust, and wheat powdery mildew. — ChEBI
