cilofungin

CHEBI:315019 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A cyclic hexapeptide echinocandin antibiotic isolated from Aspergillus spp. By inhibiting the conversion of lanosterol to ergosterol, it invades a fungus' ability to synthesize cell walls. A modified form of echinocandin B, it is an antimycotic agent against Candida albicans. — ChEBI
