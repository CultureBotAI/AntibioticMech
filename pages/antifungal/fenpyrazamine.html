fenpyrazamine

CHEBI:83327 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A pyrazolone that is pyazol-3-one which is substituted at positions 2, 4, and 5 by isopropyl, o-methylphenyl, and amino groups, respectively, and in which the hydrogen attached to the nitrogen at position 1 is replaced by an S-allyl carbothioate moiety. A fungicide, it is used for the control of Botrytis in the greenhouse cultivation of tomatoes, peppers, and cucumbers. — ChEBI
