5-Fluorouracil

CHEBI:46345 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

5-Fluorouracil (5FU) is an antimetabolite typically used as a cancer treatment and is cytotoxic. It is a pyrimidine analogue inhibits thymidylate synthase which halts the DNA synthesis. Its cytotoxic effects on eukaryotic cells have also allowed the drug to be explored for fungal infection treatment as well. — PMID:20823283
