Oteseconazole coordinates the heme iron of fungal CYP51 (Erg11) to block ergosterol biosynthesis MECHANISTIC

Oteseconazole (VT-1161) binds the active-site heme iron of fungal lanosterol 14-alpha-demethylase (CYP51 / Erg11) via its 1-tetrazole ring, inhibits 14-alpha-demethylation of sterol precursors, and depletes membrane ergosterol in Candida species.
