5-hydroxy-8-methoxy-4-phenylisoquinolin-1(2H)-one

CHEBI:141354 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An isoquinolinol that is 5-hydroxyisoquinolin-1(2H)-one which has been substituted at positions 4 and 8 by phenyl and methoxy groups, respectively. Isolated from the fermentation of an endophytic fungus Penicillium sp. R22 in Nerium indicum. It displayed weak antibacterial activity. — ChEBI
