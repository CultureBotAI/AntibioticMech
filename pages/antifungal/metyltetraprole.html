metyltetraprole

CHEBI:141152 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of tetrazoles that is 1-methyl-4-phenyltetrazole in which the phenyl group has been substituted at positions 2 and 3 by [1-(p-chlorophenyl)-1H-pyrazol-3-yl]oxy}methyl and methyl groups, respectively. A quinone outside inhibitor, it is a fungicide that can be used to control a broad range of diseases, including Septoria leaf blotch in wheat. — ChEBI
