prothiocarb

CHEBI:82084 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A monothiocarbamic ester that is ethanethiol in which the hydrogen attached to the sulfur is replaced by a [3-(dimethylamino)propyl]carbamoyl group. Formerly used (generally as the hydrochloride salt) as an agricultural fungicide, it is not approved for use within the European Union. — ChEBI
