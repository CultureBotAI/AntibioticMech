Aspergillus fumigatus sterol 14-alpha-demethylase (CYP51A / CYP51B) PROTEIN DIRECT_BINDING_TARGET

Cell-free biochemical inhibition of recombinant Aspergillus fumigatus CYP51A (IC50 = 0.23 uM) and CYP51B (IC50 = 0.22 uM), type II heme binding spectra, and cellular [14C]acetate sterol-biosynthesis inhibition (IC50 = 0.0042 ug/mL) in A. fumigatus Af293 (DOI:10.1128/AAC.02280-16; PMID:28223388).
