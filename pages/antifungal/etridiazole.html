etridiazole

CHEBI:81761 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of thiadiazoles that is 1,2,4-thiadiazole which is substituted at positions 3 and 5 by trichloromethyl and ethoxy groups, respectively. A fungicide, it has been used particularly for the control of Phytophthora and Pythium species in soils. — ChEBI
