flusulfamide

CHEBI:81801 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A sulfonamide resulting from the formal condensation 4-chloro-3-(trifluoromethyl)benzenesulfonic acid with 2-chloro-4-nitroaniline. A fungicide, it is used to control Plasmodiophora brassicae in brassicas and Polymyxa betae in sugarbeet. — ChEBI
