cyclosporin A

CHEBI:4031 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A cyclic nonribosomal peptide of eleven amino acids; an immunosuppressant drug widely used in post-allogeneic organ transplant to reduce the activity of the patient's immune system, and therefore the risk of organ rejection. Also causes reversible inhibition of immunocompetent lymphocytes in the G0- and G1-phase of the cell cycle. — ChEBI
