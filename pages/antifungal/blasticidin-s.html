Causal graphs

Blasticidin S bends P-site tRNA on the 70S ribosome to inhibit translation termination MECHANISTIC

Blasticidin S binds the 50S P-site loop of the 70S ribosome-tRNA complex, stabilizes a deformed P-site tRNA conformation toward the A site, and strongly inhibits release-factor-mediated peptidyl-tRNA hydrolysis during translation termination.
