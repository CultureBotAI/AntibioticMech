natamycin

CHEBI:7488 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A macrolide antibiotic that has formula C33H47NO13, produced by several Streptomyces species including Streptomyces natalensis. It exhibits broad spectrum antifungal activity and used in eye drops, and as a food preservative, and also as a postharvest biofungicide for citrus and other fruit crops. — ChEBI
