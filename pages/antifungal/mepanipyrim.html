mepanipyrim

CHEBI:6751 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of aminopyrimidines that is N-phenylpyrimidin-2-amine carrying additional methyl and 1-propynyl substituents at positions 4 and 6 respectively. A fungicide used to control a wide range of diseases including grey mould on strawberries, tomatoes and cucumabers, and scab on apples and pears. — ChEBI
