fenamidone

CHEBI:83258 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of imidazolones that is 3,5-dihydroimidazol-4-one substituted at position 2 by a methylthiogroup, at position 3 by an anilino group and at position 5 by phenyl and methyl groups (the S-enantiomer). A fungicide effective against Oomycete diseases such as downy mildew and certain leaf spot diseases. — ChEBI
