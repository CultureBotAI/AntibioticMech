Molecular targets

The molecular entity or process this compound acts on. Each target carries evidence — this is a mechanistic claim, not a classification.

beta-1,3-glucan synthase reaction BIOLOGICAL_PROCESS MEASURED_TARGET_ASSOCIATION

Whole-cell and cell-free experiments in Saccharomyces cerevisiae and Candida albicans measured reduced cell-wall glucan synthesis. No direct binding to a molecularly defined glucan-synthase subunit was established. Evidence status: PRIMARY_EVIDENCE. Source: PRIMARY_LITERATURE 1977-04; 1982-02 (retrieved 2026-09-02).

  • PMID:324960 (Primary whole-cell mechanism study in S. cerevisiae.)
  • PMID:7042671 (Primary whole-cell and cell-free mechanism study in C. albicans and S. cerevisiae.)

Mechanism summary

Mode of action
CELL_WALL_SYNTHESIS_INHIBITION microbial target — CURATOR: PMID:324960 showed reduced incorporation of glucose into Saccharomyces cerevisiae cell-wall glucan, and PMID:7042671 showed preferential inhibition of alkali-insoluble glucan and beta-1,3-glucan synthesis from UDP-glucose in Candida albicans and S. cerevisiae extracts. These experiments support fungal cell-wall synthesis inhibition without establishing direct binding to a defined synthase subunit.
