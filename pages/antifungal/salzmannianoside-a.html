salzmannianoside A

CHEBI:66157 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A triterpenoid saponin that consists of gypsogenin attached to a α-L-rhamnopyranosyl-(1→2)-[β-D-glucopyranosyl-(1→4)]-α-L-arabinopyranosyl] residue at position 3 via a glycosidic linkage. Isolated from Serjania salzmanniana, it exhibits antifungal and molluscicidal activities. — ChEBI
