pyrimorph

CHEBI:83663 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An enamide resulting from the formal condensation of 3-(p-tert-butylphenyl)-3-(6-chloropyridin-2-yl)acrylic acid with the amino group of morpholine. A fungicide developed in China, it shows high antifungal activity against diseases caused by Phytophthora infestans, Phytophthora capsici, Rhizoctonia solani, Peronophythora litchi, and Pseudoperonospora cubensis. — ChEBI
