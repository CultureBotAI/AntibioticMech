epichlicin

CHEBI:65849 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A homodetic cyclic peptide containing L-Pro, D-Tyr, L-Ser, L-Asn and L-Gln as the amino acid residues. Isolated from an endophytic fungus, Epichloe typhina, it exhibits inhibitory activity toward the spore germination of Cladosporium phlei, a pathogenic fungus of the timothy plant. — ChEBI
