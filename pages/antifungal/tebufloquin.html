tebufloquin

CHEBI:83266 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of quinolines that is quinoline which is substituted at positions 2 and 3 by methyl groups and at positions 4, 6, and 8 by acetoxy, tert-butyl, and fluorine, respectively. A fungicide developed mainly for the control of rice blast (Magnaporthe grisea), it has also been found to be effective against fungal diseases in soya beans and tomatoes. — ChEBI
