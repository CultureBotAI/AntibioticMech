fludioxonil

CHEBI:81763 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of benzodioxoles that is 2,2-difluoro-1,3-benzodioxole substituted at position 4 by a 3-cyanopyrrol-4-yl group. A fungicide seed treatment for control of a range of diseases including Fusarium, Rhizoctonia and Alternaria . — ChEBI
