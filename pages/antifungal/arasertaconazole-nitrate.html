arasertaconazole nitrate

CHEBI:83692 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An organic nitrate salt obtained by reaction of equimolar amounts of arasertaconazole and nitric acid. The active R-enantiomer of sertaconazole nitrate that is used for treatment of vulvovaginal candidiasis. The racemate itself is also used as a broad-spectrum antifungal drug. — ChEBI
