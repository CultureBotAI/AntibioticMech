Mechanism summary

Mode of action
OTHER microbial target — CURATOR: (5S)-Clavam natural product produced by Streptomyces clavuligerus and Streptomyces antibioticus that lacks beta-lactamase inhibitory activity due to its (5S) ring-junction stereochemistry and acts as an antifungal antimetabolite inhibiting fungal cellular metabolic pathways (Brown et al. 1979, DOI:10.1039/C39790000282; Mosher et al. 2000, DOI:10.1128/AAC.44.3.720-726.2000, PMID:10681345; Tahlan et al. 2007, DOI:10.1016/j.chembiol.2006.11.012, PMID:17317567).
