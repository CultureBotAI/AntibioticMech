antimycin A

CHEBI:2762 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A nine-membered bis-lactone having methyl substituents at the 2- and 6-positions, an n-hexyl substituent at the 8-position, an acyloxy substituent at the 7-position and an aroylamido substituent at the 3-position. It is produced by Streptomyces bacteria and has found commercial use as a fish poison. — ChEBI
