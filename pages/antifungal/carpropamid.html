carpropamid

CHEBI:3434 ·resolve ·ANTIFUNGAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

A cyclopropylcarboxamide obtained by formal condensation of the carboxy group of 2,2-dichloro-1-ethyl-3-methylcyclopropanecarboxylic acid with the amino group of 1-(4-chlorophenyl)ethylamine. A rice fungicide with specific action against Pyricularia oryzae. It is not highly toxic to mammals but shows a moderate level of toxicity to birds, fish and earthworms. — ChEBI

Carpropamid binds fungal scytalone dehydratase to inhibit 1,8-dihydroxynaphthalene melanin biosynthesis MECHANISTIC

Carpropamid binds tightly inside the narrow hydrophobic active-site cavity of Pyricularia oryzae scytalone dehydratase (SDH1), displaces the catalytic water molecules required for dehydration of scytalone and vermelone, and blocks fungal 1,8-dihydroxynaphthalene melanin biosynthesis.
