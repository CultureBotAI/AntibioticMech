celecoxib

CHEBI:41423 ·resolve ·ANTIMYCOBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of pyrazoles that is 1H-pyrazole which is substituted at positions 1, 3 and 5 by 4-sulfamoylphenyl, trifluoromethyl and p-tolyl groups, respectively. A cyclooxygenase-2 inhibitor, it is used in the treatment of arthritis. — ChEBI
