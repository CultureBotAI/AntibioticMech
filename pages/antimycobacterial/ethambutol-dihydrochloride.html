ethambutol dihydrochloride

CHEBI:4878 ·resolve ·ANTIMYCOBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

The dihydrchloride salt of ethambutol. A bacteriostatic antimycobacterial drug, it is effective against Mycobacterium tuberculosis and some other mycobacteria. It is used in combination with other antituberculous drugs in the treatment of pulmonary and extrapulmonary tuberculosis; resistant strains of M. tuberculosis are readily produced if ethambutol dihydrochloride is used alone. — ChEBI
