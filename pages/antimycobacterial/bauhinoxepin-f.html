bauhinoxepin F

CHEBI:65470 ·resolve ·ANTIMYCOBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A dibenzooxepine that is 10,11-dihydrodibenzo[b,f]oxepine substituted by methoxy groups at positions 6 and 9, methyl group at position 7 and hydroxy groups at positions 1 and 8 respectively. It is isolated from the root extract of Bauhinia purpurea and exhibits antimalarial, antimycobacterial, antifungal, anti-inflammatory and cytotoxic activities. — ChEBI
