(R)-N-trans-feruloyloctopamine

CHEBI:67373 ·resolve ·ANTIMYCOBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of cinnamamides that is an enamide obtained by the formal condensation of the amino group of (R)-octopamine with the carboxy group of ferulic acid. Isolated from Pisonia aculeata, it exhibits antitubercular activity. — ChEBI
