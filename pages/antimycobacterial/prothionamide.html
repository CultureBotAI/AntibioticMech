Prothionamide

CHEBI:32066 ·resolve ·ANTIMYCOBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

Prothionamide is a thioamide derivative with antibacterial properties. It increases cell wall permeability and decreases cell wall damage resistance by inhibition of mycolic acid synthesis, resulting in cell death. It is particularly used to treat M. tuberculosis and M. leprae infections. — PMID:6459296
