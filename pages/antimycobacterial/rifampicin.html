Mechanism summary

Mode of action
NUCLEIC_ACID_SYNTHESIS_INHIBITION host shared target — CURATOR: PMID:11290327 resolved rifampicin bound to Thermus aquaticus core RNA polymerase. It occupies a beta-subunit pocket in the DNA/RNA channel more than 12 angstrom from the active site and sterically blocks extension once nascent RNA reaches two to three nucleotides. This primary transcription mechanism replaces the source-seeded MULTIPLE bucket.
