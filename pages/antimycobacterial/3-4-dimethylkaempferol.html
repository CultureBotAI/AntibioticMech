3,4'-dimethylkaempferol

CHEBI:146142 ·resolve ·ANTIMYCOBACTERIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A dimethoxyflavone that is kaempferol in which the hydroxy groups at position 3 and 4' have been replaced by methoxy groups. It is a component of bee glue and isolated from several plant species including Tanacetum microphyllum. — ChEBI
