Method and provenance

Model morgan-count-chiral-r2_0.90-r4_0.10+tanimoto+umap-precomputed-n15-d0.05-c2-rs42. The generator parses stored SMILES first and falls back to standard InChI when RDKit rejects the stored SMILES. This build used that fallback for 176 records and retained 202 multi-fragment records. All 189 detected stereoisomer pairs have nonzero model distance.

Distance is 90% radius-2 and 10% radius-4 chiral Morgan count-fingerprint Tanimoto distance. Coordinates are generated with UMAP(metric=precomputed, n_neighbors=15, min_dist=0.05, random_state=42). A corpus or model-version change can move every point.

Most of this corpus is machine-generated and unreviewed. Identity, structure and cross-references come from ChEBI and CARD's own data; the mechanism layer — molecular_targets, mode_of_action, causal_graphs — is curated incrementally. Treat any record marked SEEDED as a starting point, not a fully reviewed claim. Current review counts.
