ribavirin 5'-triphosphate

CHEBI:45578 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

A 1-ribosyltriazole that is ribavirin in which the hydroxy group at the 5'-position is replaced by a triphosphate group. It is the active metabolite of the antiviral agent ribavirin. — ChEBI

Mechanism summary

Mode of action
MULTIPLE microbial target — CURATOR: the exact triphosphate has more than one experimentally supported viral-enzyme interaction. PMID:15152003 directly structures competitive binding to dengue NS5 2'-O-methyltransferase; PMID:11833782 measures inhibition of HCV NTPase/helicase; and PMID:12606760 tests ribavirin nucleotides against HCV polymerase. The prior polymerase-only seed was therefore too narrow. Published eIF4E results conflict and are kept as an open controversy rather than asserted as a target.

Discussions

Open questions, knowledge gaps and curation todos attached to this compound.

CONTROVERSY OPEN

Does ribavirin 5'-triphosphate bind eIF4E with physiologically relevant affinity and inhibit cap-dependent translation?

PMID:16131589 reported weak binding and no eIF4E-specific translation inhibition in vitro, whereas PMID:23583375 reported concentration-dependent NMR evidence for a higher-affinity complex. Resolving the assay-dependent discrepancy would determine whether a host target belongs in the curated mechanism.
