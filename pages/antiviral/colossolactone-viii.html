colossolactone VIII

CHEBI:65651 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A tetracyclic triterpenoid that is A,B-dihomo-19-nor-4-oxalanosta-1,8,19,24-tetraen-26,22-olide substituted by an acetoxy group at position 15, a hydroxy group at position 23 and an oxo group at position 3 (the 22S, 23R stereoisomer). It is isolated from the fruiting bodies of the Vietnamese mushroom Ganoderma colossum and displays inhibitory activity towards the enzyme HIV protease. — ChEBI
