tilorone

CHEBI:147347 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of fluoren-9-ones that is 9H-fluoren-9-one which is substituted by a 2-(diethylamino)ethoxy group at positions 2 and 7. It is an interferon inducer and a selective α7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor (α7 nAChR) agonist. Its hydrochloride salt is used as an antiviral drug. — ChEBI
