16β,17-dihydroxy-ent-kaurane-19-oic acid

CHEBI:65778 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A ent-kaurane diterpenoid that is ent-kaurane-19-oic acid substituted by hydroxy groups at positions 16 and 17 (the 16β stereoisomer). It is isolated from Helianthus sp. and Annona squamosa and exhibits anti-HIV activity. — ChEBI
