harman

CHEBI:5623 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

An indole alkaloid fundamental parent with a structure of 9H-β-carboline carrying a methyl substituent at C-1. It has been isolated from the bark of Sickingia rubra, Symplocus racemosa, Passiflora incarnata, Peganum harmala, Banisteriopsis caapi and Tribulus terrestris, as well as from tobacco smoke. It is a specific, reversible inhibitor of monoamine oxidase A. — ChEBI
