ginkgetin

CHEBI:5353 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A biflavonoid that is the 7,4'-dimethyl ether derivative of amentoflavone. Isolated from Ginkgo biloba and Dioon, it exhibits anti-HSV-1, antineoplastic and inhibitory activities towards arachidonate 5-lipoxygenase and cyclooxygenase 2. — ChEBI
