colossolactone V

CHEBI:65649 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A tricyclic triterpenoid that is 3,4-secolanosta-8,24-diene-3,26-dioic acid 3-methylester substituted by acetoxy groups at positions 19 and 22 and a hydroxy group at position 4. It is isolated from the fruiting bodies of the Vietnamese mushroom Ganoderma colossum and displays inhibitory activity towards the enzyme HIV protease. — ChEBI
