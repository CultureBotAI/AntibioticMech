pedunculosumoside C

CHEBI:67875 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A homoflavonoid glycoside that is 5'-prenylophioglonol attached to 2-O-β-D-glucopyranosyl-β-D-glucopyranosyl residue at position 7 via glycosidic linkage. It has been isolated from the whole plant of Ophioglossum pedunculosum and exhibits anti-HBV activity. — ChEBI
