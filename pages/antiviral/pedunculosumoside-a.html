pedunculosumoside A

CHEBI:67873 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A homoflavonoid glycoside that is ophioglonol substituted by a prenyl group at position 5' as well as 2-O-(β-D-glucopyranosyl)-β-D-glucopyranosyl and β-D-glucopyranosyl residues at positions 7 and 4' respectively via glycosidic linkages. Isolated from the whole plants of Ophioglossum pedunculosum, it exhibits anti-HBV activity. — ChEBI
