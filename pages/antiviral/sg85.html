SG85

CHEBI:147346 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

A secondary carboxamide resulting from the formal condensation of the carboxy group of N-[(benzyloxy)carbonyl]-O-tert-butyl-L-serine with the primary amino group of ethyl (2E,4S)-5-[(3S)-2-oxopyrrolidin-3-yl]-4-(L-phenylalanylamino)pent-2-enoate. It is a potent enterovirus 3C protease inhibitor with EC50 of 180 nM against enterovirus 71 (EV71) and 60 nM against human rhinovirus 14 in a live virus-cell-based assay. — ChEBI
