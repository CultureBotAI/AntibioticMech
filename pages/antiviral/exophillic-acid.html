Mechanism summary

Mode of action
MULTIPLE — CURATOR: exophillic acid has two compound-specific antiviral mechanisms in different systems. PMID:15015729 measured inhibition of HIV-1 integrase strand transfer (IC50 68 micromolar), whereas PMID:35458494 found that it interacts with host NTCP and blocks preS1-mediated attachment and entry of HBV and HDV without affecting intracellular HBV replication. The mixed viral and host targets make a single target-scope value inappropriate.

Exophillic acid inhibits HIV-1 integrase strand transfer and blocks NTCP-mediated HBV/HDV entry MECHANISTIC

Exophillic acid exhibits two compound-specific antiviral mechanisms in distinct viral systems: it inhibits HIV-1 integrase strand-transfer catalysis in vitro and interacts with the human hepatocyte receptor sodium taurocholate cotransporting polypeptide (NTCP) to block preS1-mediated attachment and entry of hepatitis B and hepatitis D viruses.
