cytarabine

CHEBI:28680 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A pyrimidine nucleoside in which cytosine is attached to D-arabinofuranose via a β-N1-glycosidic bond. Used mainly in the treatment of leukaemia, especially acute non-lymphoblastic leukaemia, cytarabine is an antimetabolite antineoplastic agent that inhibits the synthesis of DNA. It also has antiviral and immunosuppressant properties. — ChEBI
