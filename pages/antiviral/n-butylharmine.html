N-butylharmine

CHEBI:66002 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of β-carbolines that is 9H-β-carboline substituted by a butyl group at position 9, methoxy group at position 7 and a methyl group at position 1. It is semisynthetic derivative of harmine and has been shown to exhibit significant anti-HIV activity. — ChEBI
