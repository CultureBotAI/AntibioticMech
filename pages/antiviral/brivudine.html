brivudine

CHEBI:47278 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A pyrimidine 2'-deoxyribonucleoside that is 2'-deoxyuridine in which the hydrogen at position 5 of the uracil ring is substituted by a 2-bromoethenyl group. It is an antiviral drug used for the treatment of herpes zoster (shingles). — ChEBI
