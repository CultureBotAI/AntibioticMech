favipiravir-RTP

CHEBI:170009 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

A nuceloside analogue in which the nucleobase is favipiravir. It is a RNA-dependent RNA polymerase inhibitor which exhibits anti-influenza and anti-coronaviral activities. — ChEBI
