niruriside

An O-acyl carbohydrate that consists of β-D-fructofuranosyl β-D-glucopyranoside in which the hydroxy protons are replaced by acetyl and trans-cinnamoyl groups. Isolated from Phyllanthus niruri, it exhibits anti-HIV activity. — ChEBI
