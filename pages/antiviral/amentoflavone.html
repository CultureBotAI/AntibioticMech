amentoflavone

CHEBI:2631 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A biflavonoid that is obtained by oxidative coupling of two molecules of apigenin resulting in a bond between positions C-3 of the hydroxyphenyl ring and C-8 of the chromene ring. A natural product found particularly in Ginkgo biloba and Hypericum perforatum. — ChEBI
