7,4'-di-O-galloyltricetiflavan

CHEBI:65944 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A diester obtained by the formal condensation of gallic acid with the hydroxy groups at positions 7 and 4' of 3',4',5',5,7-pentahydroxyflavan. Isolated from the leaves of Pithecellobium clypearia, it exhibits antiviral activity. — ChEBI
