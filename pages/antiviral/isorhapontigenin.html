isorhapontigenin

CHEBI:167830 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A stilbenol that is trans-stilbene in which one of the phenyl groups is substituted by hydroxy groups at positions 3 and 5, while the other phenyl group is substituted by a methoxy group at position 3 and a hydroxy group at position 4. It natural product isolated from the seeds of Aïphanes aculeata and has antioxidant, anti-tumour, and anti-inflammatory effects. — ChEBI
