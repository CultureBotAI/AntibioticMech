N2-methylharmine

CHEBI:66005 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of β-carbolines that is 9H-β-carboline substituted by a methoxy group at position 7 and methyl groups at positions 1 and 2. It is a semisynthetic derivative of harmine and has been shown to exhibit significant anti-HIV activity. — ChEBI
