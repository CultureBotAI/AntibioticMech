1β-hydroxymaprounic acid 3-p-hydroxybenzoate

CHEBI:66671 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A pentacyclic triterpenoid that is the benzoate ester obtained by the condensation of the 3-hydroxy group of 1β-hydroxymaprounic acid with p-hydroxybenzoic acid. Isolated from Maprounea africana, it exhibits inhibitory activity against HIV-1 reverse transcriptase. — ChEBI
