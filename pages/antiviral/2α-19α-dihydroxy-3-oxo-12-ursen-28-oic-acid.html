2α,19α-dihydroxy-3-oxo-12-ursen-28-oic acid

CHEBI:65783 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A pentacyclic triterpenoid that is urs-12-ene substituted by a carboxy group at position 28, α-configured hydroxy groups at positions 2 and 19 and an oxo group at position 3. Isolated from the whole plants of Geum japonicum, it exhibits activity against HIV-1 protease. — ChEBI
