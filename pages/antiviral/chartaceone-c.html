(±)-chartaceone C

CHEBI:68935 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A dihydroxyflavanone that is flavanone substituted by hydroxy groups at positions 5 and 7 and 6-carboxy-1-phenylhex-2-en-1-yl groups at positions 6 and 8 respectively. It has been isolated as a racemate from the bark of Cryptocarya chartacea and exhibits inhibitory activity against dengue virus NS5 polymerase. — ChEBI
