favipiravir

CHEBI:134722 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

A member of the class of pyrazines that is pyrazine substituted by aminocarbonyl, hydroxy and fluoro groups at positions 2, 3 and 6, respectively. It is an anti-viral agent that inhibits RNA-dependent RNA polymerase of several RNA viruses and is approved for the treatment of influenza in Japan. — ChEBI
