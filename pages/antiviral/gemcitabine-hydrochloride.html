gemcitabine hydrochloride

CHEBI:31647 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A 2'-deoxycytidine hydrochloriode having geminal fluoro substituents in the 2'-position. An inhibitor of ribonucleotide reductase, gemcitabine hydrochloride is used in the treatment of various carcinomas, including non-small cell lung cancer, pancreatic cancer, bladder cancer and breast cancer. — ChEBI
