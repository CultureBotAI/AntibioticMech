12-deoxyphorbol-13-(3E,5E-decadienoate)

CHEBI:65747 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A phorbol ester that consists of 12-deoxyphorbol esterified at position 13 by deca-3,5-dienoic acid (the 3E,5E stereoisomer). It is obtained from the leaves and stems of Excoecaria agallocha and exhibits anti-HIV activity. — ChEBI
