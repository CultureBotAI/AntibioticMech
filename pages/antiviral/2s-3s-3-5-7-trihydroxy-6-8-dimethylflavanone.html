(2S,3S)-3,5,7-trihydroxy-6,8-dimethylflavanone

CHEBI:70653 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A trihydroxyflavanone that is flavanone substituted by hydroxy groups at positions 3, 5 and 7 and methyl groups at positions 6 and 8 (the 2S,3S-stereoisomer). It has been isolated from the buds of Cleistocalyx operculatus and has been shown to exhibit inhibitory effects on the viral neuraminidases from two influenza viral strains, H1N1 and H9N2. — ChEBI
