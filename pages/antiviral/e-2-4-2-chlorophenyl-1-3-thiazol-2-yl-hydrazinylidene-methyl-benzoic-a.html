(E)-2-[[[4-(2-chlorophenyl)-1,3-thiazol-2-yl]hydrazinylidene]methyl]benzoic acid

CHEBI:167669 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A member of the class of 1,3-thiazoles that is 1,3-thiazole substituted by (2E)-2-(2-carboxybenzylidene)hydrazinyl and 2-chlorophenyl groups at positions 2 and 4, respectively. It is a potent inhibitor of human dihydroorotate dehydrogenase (hDHODH) with IC50 of 32 nM. It exhibits antineoplastic activity and broad-spectrum antiviral activity against various RNA viruses including influenza A, Zika, Ebola, and SARS-CoV-2 viruses. — ChEBI
