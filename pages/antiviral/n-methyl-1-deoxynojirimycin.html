N-methyl-1-deoxynojirimycin

CHEBI:166564 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A hydroxypiperidine that is duvoglustat in which the amino hydrogen is replaced by a methyl group. It is an inhibitor of α-glucosidase, an agonist of the glucose sensor SGLT3 and exhibits anti-HIV activity. — ChEBI
