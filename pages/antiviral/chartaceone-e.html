(±)-chartaceone E

CHEBI:68937 ·resolve ·ANTIVIRAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A dihydroxyflavanone that is flavanone substituted by hydroxy groups at positions 5 and 7, a 6-carboxy-1-phenylhex-2-en-1-yl group at position 8 and 6-carboxy-1-phenylhex-2-en-3-yl groups at position 6. It has been isolated as a racemate from the bark of Cryptocarya chartacea and exhibits inhibitory activity against dengue virus NS5 polymerase. — ChEBI
