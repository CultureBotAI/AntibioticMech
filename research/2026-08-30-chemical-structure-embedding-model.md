# Chemical-structure embedding decision for the antibiotic map

- **Decision date:** 2026-08-30
- **Corpus commit:** `c93b3e40`
- **Corpus size:** 2,923 records
- **Decision:** use a multi-scale chiral Morgan count representation with a
  weighted Tanimoto distance; do not use a neural SMILES embedding as the
  primary chemical map.

## Decision

The primary chemical view should use two unhashed, sparse, count-based Morgan
fingerprints generated by RDKit:

- radius 2, with chirality (`ECFP4` scale);
- radius 4, with chirality (`ECFP8` scale).

Pairwise chemical distance is:

```text
d(i, j) = 0.90 * (1 - Tanimoto(Morgan-r2(i), Morgan-r2(j)))
        + 0.10 * (1 - Tanimoto(Morgan-r4(i), Morgan-r4(j)))
```

The radius-2 channel supplies the useful local functional-group and scaffold
neighborhoods. The smaller radius-4 channel resolves stereochemical cases whose
locally symmetric environments remain tied at radius 2. On this corpus the
combination retained the best radius-2 neighborhood metrics while eliminating
all zero-distance pairs among the 189 observed stereoisomer pairs.

This is a chemical fingerprint representation rather than a neural foundation
model. That is deliberate. For an unsupervised map of molecular structure, the
benchmark did not find a learned model that improved the chemical neighborhoods
enough to justify its instability, compute, and coverage limitations.

The 2D view should fit UMAP to the precomputed distance matrix with a fixed
configuration, initially:

```text
metric       = precomputed
n_neighbors  = 15
min_dist     = 0.05
n_components = 2
random_state = 42
```

The visible page can color, filter, and label points with antimicrobial class,
mechanism, target, producer, or review status. None of those fields may enter
the chemical distance. That is what keeps this view distinct from the text or
knowledge-graph embedding view.

## Required structure input path

Using the stored SMILES alone does not cover the corpus with RDKit 2026.03.5.
RDKit rejected 176 stored SMILES during kekulization, including daptomycin and
several peptide, indole, nucleoside, and heteroaromatic records. Every one was
recovered from the record's standard InChI.

The production parse contract is therefore:

```python
mol = Chem.MolFromSmiles(record["chemical_structure"]["smiles"])
if mol is None:
    mol = Chem.MolFromInchi(record["chemical_structure"]["standard_inchi"])
if mol is None:
    raise StructureEmbeddingError(record["identifier"])
```

At the audited commit this produces 2,923/2,923 molecules. The artifact must
record which input path was used; silently omitting the 176 InChI fallbacks
would disproportionately remove structurally interesting natural products.

The primary view should embed the exact record structure, including charge and
defined counterions, because that is the repository's identity unit. It should
not silently neutralize, desalt, or select a largest fragment. Clear mixture and
combination-product records identified in issue
[#90](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/AntibioticMech/issues/90) should be
excluded or remodeled at the corpus layer rather than chemically rewritten by
the visualization.

## Corpus-specific benchmark

### Evaluation design

Every candidate was evaluated on all 2,923 structures where its interface
allowed it. The test used:

- precision at 10 for the same `structural_class_id` among the 569 annotated
  records;
- precision at 10 for a shared `parent_compounds` identifier among 2,911
  records;
- precision at 10 for an identical chiral Bemis--Murcko scaffold among 1,520
  records with a repeated scaffold;
- zero-distance rate across 189 pairs sharing connectivity but carrying
  different isomeric SMILES;
- UMAP trustworthiness at 10 and high-dimensional/2D neighbor overlap at 10;
- coverage, runtime, behavior across molecular-size quartiles, and qualitative
  neighbors for erythromycin A, vancomycin, ciprofloxacin, tetracycline,
  fluconazole, zidovudine, and daptomycin.

The internal class, parent, and scaffold labels are imperfect proxies rather
than a gold-standard similarity judgment. They are useful here because all
three are chemical and independent of record prose. Antimicrobial-class purity
was measured only as a secondary diagnostic and was not used to choose the
model, since activity is biological rather than structural.

and exclude YAML parsing. MoLFormer's all-pairs cosine calculation took only

Morgan representation needed about 11.9 seconds for both complete pairwise
Tanimoto matrices after its 0.23-second feature pass.

The selected view produced chemically coherent local examples:

- erythromycin A neighbored erythromycin C, clarithromycin, labeled
  erythromycin, and close erythromycin derivatives;
- vancomycin neighbored norvancomycin, chloroeremomycin, chloroorienticin B,
  devancoaminyl vancomycin, and balhimycin;
- ciprofloxacin neighbored its salt/hydrate records, enrofloxacin, norfloxacin,
  and sulfociprofloxacin;
- tetracycline neighbored chlortetracycline, oxytetracycline, rolitetracycline,
  its zwitterion, and lymecycline;
- fluconazole neighbored other triazole antifungals;
- zidovudine neighbored related antiviral nucleosides;
- daptomycin's neighborhood contained other peptide antibiotics rather than
  small-molecule mechanism analogues.

### Stereochemistry result

An achiral radius-2 Morgan fingerprint collapsed 188 of the 189 stereoisomer
pairs. Setting `includeChirality=True` reduced this to seven pairs at radius 2.
The remaining cases were 5-episisomicin/sisomycin and specified/unspecified or
R/S bromazine records. Radius 3 resolved the sisomicin pair; radius 4 resolved
all seven. This is why the selected distance retains a small radius-4 channel
rather than relying on a folded radius-2 bit vector.

## Learned-model comparison

### IBM MoLFormer

MoLFormer was the learned model tested end to end because its official
Apache-2.0 checkpoint is explicitly intended for frozen feature extraction,
similarity, and visualization. It encoded all records after RDKit
canonicalization, but it was not the better chemical map:

- its structural-class, parent, scaffold, and 2D-neighbor metrics were all
  below the selected Morgan representation;
- 58 corpus structures exceed the model's published 202-token training limit;
- the model's training canonicalized SMILES with isomeric information removed;
- two valid randomized SMILES for the same molecule had median cosine
  similarity 0.805, fifth-percentile similarity 0.635, and a minimum of 0.006
  across a 200-molecule test.

The last result is disqualifying for a canonical chemical map: a molecule's
location should not depend strongly on the arbitrary graph traversal used to
write its SMILES. MoLFormer remains reasonable for a future learned-property
or transfer-learning experiment, but not as the authoritative structure view.

The official model card also states both relevant limitations: isomeric
information was removed during training, and sequences longer than 202 tokens
were excluded. See the
[IBM MoLFormer model card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-research/MoLFormer-XL-both-10pct)
and the [original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09553).

### MAP4

MAP4 was a strong paper candidate because it was designed to span drugs,
natural products, peptides, and biomolecules. That motivation fits this corpus,
whose heavy-atom counts range from 2 to 339. The standard implementation is
achiral, however, and the corpus benchmark exposed severe ties and implausible
small-molecule neighborhoods. It collapsed 97 of 189 stereoisomer pairs and was
well below Morgan on every primary neighborhood and projection metric.

The result does not refute MAP4's published use for very large natural-product
spaces; it says it is not the best single representation for this mixed
2,923-record corpus. See the
[MAP4 paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7291580/) and the
[natural-product application](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7600738/).

### Uni-Mol and Uni-Mol2

Uni-Mol is attractive for a genuinely three-dimensional view, but that would be
a different scientific product. The corpus stores no curated conformer ensemble.
`unimol_tools` generates one RDKit conformer, falls back to 2D coordinates or
zeros when 3D generation fails, and defaults to a 256-atom limit with random
cropping above it. This corpus includes 202 multi-fragment structures and
molecules with up to 339 heavy atoms before hydrogens, so the generated geometry
would be an algorithmic choice rather than an observed structure.

Use Uni-Mol later only if the project creates a separately labeled 3D-conformer
view with explicit protonation, conformer generation, failure, cropping, and
ensemble provenance. It should not replace the exact 2D topology view. The
[official Uni-Mol repository](https://github.com/deepmodeling/Uni-Mol) is MIT
licensed and documents the available representation models.

### Other neural candidates

- ChemBERTa-2 and similar SMILES encoders share the SMILES-enumeration and
  stereochemistry concerns and were not a stronger deployment candidate than
  MoLFormer.
- The 2025 comparison of 25 pretrained molecular embedding models across 25
  datasets found negligible or no improvement over ECFP for almost every neural
  model. That external result is consistent with this corpus benchmark:
  [Praski et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06199).
- CLAMP's molecular encoder is trained jointly against assay text. It is useful
  for biological retrieval but would leak activity semantics into a view whose
  defining requirement is chemical separation from text.
- Recursion's graph foundation model named MolE is released under
  CC-BY-NC-4.0, which is a poor fit for an unrestricted public build pipeline.
- The separate MIT-licensed antimicrobial MolE implementation is relevant for
  antimicrobial-activity prediction, but its released representation was
  pretrained on a much smaller PubChem sample and uses an older PyTorch
  Geometric stack. The map does not need its predictive bias. See the
  [antimicrobial representation paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11986102/).

## Production contract

The chemical map artifact should contain, for each record:

```text
identifier
path or public URL
label                     # display only
canonical_isomeric_smiles # provenance/debugging
structure_input           # SMILES or INCHI_FALLBACK
umap_x
umap_y
nearest_neighbors         # identifier + combined chemical distance
fingerprint_version
rdkit_version
projection_version
```

Recommended version string:

```text
morgan-count-chiral-r2_0.90-r4_0.10+tanimoto+umap15-0.05-rs42
```

The build must fail if:

- any in-scope record cannot be parsed from both SMILES and InChI;
- the coordinate/neighbor artifact has a missing or unexpected identifier;
- non-structure record fields influence the feature vectors or distances;
- a representation or projection configuration changes without changing its
  version and regenerating the full artifact;
- generated output is stale relative to a hash of record identifiers,
  structures, RDKit version, fingerprint parameters, and projection parameters.

Suggested verification tests:

1. coverage is exactly the corpus lockfile set;
2. SMILES randomization leaves Morgan fingerprints and pairwise distances
   unchanged;
3. every observed stereoisomer pair has nonzero combined distance;
4. all duplicate InChIKey groups and multi-fragment records are explicitly
   reported in map metadata;
5. high-dimensional/2D neighbor overlap at 10 remains at least 0.45 and
   trustworthiness at 10 remains at least 0.95;
6. named canaries retain chemically sensible neighborhoods;
7. a build with labels, definitions, mechanisms, and classes blanked produces
   byte-identical features, distances, and coordinates.

## Bottom line

Use the multi-scale chiral Morgan count/Tanimoto representation for the distinct
chemical map. It is more chemically faithful on this corpus than the tested
learned embedding, fully deterministic, fast on CPU, permissively implementable
with RDKit, independent of text, and explicit about stereochemistry. Treat a
future Uni-Mol representation as a separate 3D conformer view, not as a
replacement for this exact-structure map.
